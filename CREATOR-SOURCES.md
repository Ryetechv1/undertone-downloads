## Find a creator-provided file and import it

1. Open a supplied playlist and choose the video by its title.
2. Tap the title or **Details**, then expand the **description** with **more**. Check the creator's pinned comment or comments if the description does not contain a file link.
3. **Where the creator provides a free MP3 or WAV**, use that creator's own download link and terms. A free YouTube stream does not itself establish a free downloadable file. A paid, account-gated, missing or unavailable file remains a reference unless you choose to obtain it under the creator's terms.
4. Save the file to **Files** on iPhone or **Downloads** on Android. A YouTube offline download stays in YouTube and cannot be imported as a local audio file.
5. Return to Undertone, choose an empty slot or **Import audio**, and select the saved MP3/WAV. Slots **6–10** let you keep the existing five starters.

No audio was downloaded, extracted or converted during the source verification. This guide supplies no YouTube-ripping instructions and no promise of a medical or biological result.
