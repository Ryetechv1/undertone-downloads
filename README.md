## Choose your phone

- **iPhone, iOS 17+:** use `Undertone-iOS.ipa` and your own Apple Account to sign
  it. Free signing requires renewal every seven days. On Windows, hold **Shift**
  when clicking AltServer's tray icon and choose **Sideload .ipa…**. Follow
  [IOS-INSTALL.md](IOS-INSTALL.md) for the complete Windows or Mac instructions.
- **Android 9+:** open the publisher-signed `Undertone-Android.apk` and follow
  [ANDROID-INSTALL.md](ANDROID-INSTALL.md). Keep Android System WebView and/or
  Chrome updated. You do not sign the APK yourself.

The same IPA targets iPhone 14, 14 Plus, 14 Pro, 14 Pro Max and other supported
iPhones on iOS 17+. These are compatibility targets, not a claim that every
physical phone model was tested.

## Start listening and customize the studio

Fresh installations show the five starter titles immediately; first Play
prepares a private native copy. Existing mixes take precedence during updates.
Use slots 6–10 for your own additions. [APP-GUIDE.md](APP-GUIDE.md) explains the
ten channels, tone generation up to 20,000 Hz, stereo positioning, larger portal
vortex, links and intent-led Saved HUD.

Gallery holds **50 items**, including up to **ten videos of 90 seconds or less**.
You can keep **five timed backgrounds**. Readable channel-cover images can be up
to **20 MB**; their bytes are stored separately from mix settings, with the
legacy saved record retained for recovery. HUD captures save PNG images into
the local Gallery.

Open **Guide** in the app or [CREATOR-SOURCES.md](CREATOR-SOURCES.md) for the two
supplied playlists and five optional references. Where a creator offers a free
MP3/WAV, use the creator's own link and terms, save it to Files / Downloads and
import the file. Direct/free-file availability remains unverified; a free stream
or a YouTube offline save does not establish an importable audio file.

## Keep your library

Keep the current app installed for an in-place update and follow your platform's
update instructions. Retain original media files outside the app. Deleting the
app, clearing its storage or changing signing identity can remove or separate
its local library.

The shared starters and starting artwork are public installer contents. Later
imports, Gallery uploads, intent text and saved mixes stay in each recipient's
local app. Private backups, credentials, pairing records and signing private
keys are excluded from this download kit. App source remains private.

Start with [START-HERE.md](START-HERE.md). The release's `SHA256SUMS.txt` and
`RELEASE-VERIFICATION.json` identify the exact installers supplied with the kit.
