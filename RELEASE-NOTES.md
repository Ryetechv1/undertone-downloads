# Undertone 3.1, build 6 — release preparation

The public guide is available. The new 3.1 starter installers are being built and checked; download controls remain inactive until the final IPA, APK, friends ZIP and verification files are published. Exact app download sizes are pending.

## Included capture

The five assigned MP3s were captured from the owner's iPhone setup and total **87,924,242 bytes** (about 88 MB) before app packaging.

- Slot 1: 24 minutes 24.764 seconds.
- Slots 2–5: 10 minutes 11.998 seconds each.
- Capture note: slots 2–5 have different titles but byte-identical MP3 contents. The starter pack preserves those captured assignments.

A fresh installation displays all five titles immediately. First Play prepares a private local copy for that slot. Pause or Clear cancels queued playback. Updates preserve the existing assignments, edits and saved mix rather than replacing them with the starter pack. On Android, preparing all five recordings eventually adds about 1.5 GB of private decoded audio, beyond the installed app and retained installer.

The starter recordings will be public as part of the downloadable installers. New personal imports, gallery content, intent text, saved mixes and credentials are local to each recipient's installation.

## Verification boundaries

The earlier 3.0 app was confirmed by its owner to install successfully, retain their library, continue playback with the phone locked, and play alongside the other audio app they tested. Those are user-reported physical iPhone results for 3.0; the new 3.1 starter candidate has not completed its build/device verification yet.

Target requirements remain iOS 17 or later on a supported iPhone, including the iPhone 14 / 14 Plus / 14 Pro / 14 Pro Max family, and Android 9 or later with updated Android System WebView and/or Chrome. Actual final build and responsive-layout results will be recorded when supplied.
