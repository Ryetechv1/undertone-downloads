Undertone 3.2 — start here

Undertone is a private media studio with ten audio slots, a visual connection network, a media gallery, rotating backgrounds, the intent-led Teal Apparitions HUD and a built-in creator-source Guide tab.

Undertone 3.2, build 9 includes the five starter recordings and expanded studio. The iPhone installer requires your own Apple signing; the Android installer is signed. RELEASE-NOTES.md and the verification record describe the actual completed checks and remaining limits.

Prepared November 11, 2026 (America/Denver). The public guide shows download availability, and the release notes record the completed checks.

New in 3.2

The updated studio restores the previous large, soft portal vortex behind the audio cards, while keeping the small central intent image and editable routes. It adds intent-led HUD color and motion with continuous visibility, a white 64-cell tetrahedral grid around the centered Core, balanced starting channel positions, Clear links / Undo clear, and Capture image for saving a PNG of the current HUD into Gallery. The separate Aether art layer has been removed.

Open Studio, Gallery, Saved HUD or Guide from the top navigation. The Guide tab keeps the two supplied playlists and five optional references together, with steps for importing a creator-provided MP3/WAV where one is offered. Read CREATOR-SOURCES.md for the verified references and availability limits.

Build 9 also changes channel-cover storage: picture bytes are saved separately from the mix settings, so changing volume or seeking does not rewrite the cover image. The update keeps the older saved record for recovery while migrating readable covers. Keep the existing app installed during the update and retain your original media files. Channel covers accept readable images up to 20 MB.

Five included recordings

A fresh installation of Undertone 3.2, build 9 starts with the five recordings captured from the owner's assigned iPhone mix. All five titles appear immediately. The first time you press Play for a recording, the app prepares its private local copy before playback. That preparation happens on the phone; you do not need to upload or stream the track.

If you press Pause or Clear while a recording is preparing, its queued playback is canceled. It will not start later just because preparation finishes. Your own channel edits and saved mix remain in place when you update. The starter pack is applied only to a fresh installation, so an update does not replace an existing mix.

The five MP3s total about 88 MB before app packaging. Exact app download sizes and hashes are included in the release verification record. Android also prepares decoded audio on disk: after all five tracks have been prepared, allow about 1.5 GB of additional private audio storage, on top of the installed app and any installer you keep.

Check a download

The package's SHA256SUMS.txt (provided with the release) lists the actual app-file hashes. These commands calculate a hash for comparison; they do not install anything:

Get-FileHash .\Undertone-iOS.ipa -Algorithm SHA256
Get-FileHash .\Undertone-Android.apk -Algorithm SHA256

On macOS:

shasum -a 256 Undertone-iOS.ipa
shasum -a 256 Undertone-Android.apk

A matching hash confirms that a file matches the supplied manifest; it does not prove device compatibility or replace signing. After installation, follow APP-GUIDE.md for importing media and testing playback.

Install Undertone 3.2 on iPhone

Use Undertone-iOS.ipa on an iPhone with iOS 17 or later, with AltServer / AltStore Classic. AltStore PAL is a different distribution route. The main installation steps were checked against the official sources on November 9, 2026 (Denver). The app file must be present in your package before starting.

The release targets iPhone 14, iPhone 14 Plus, iPhone 14 Pro and iPhone 14 Pro Max, along with other supported iPhones running iOS 17 or later. Install the same IPA on these models; a separate model-specific download is not needed.

The 3.2 starter release includes five assigned MP3s totaling about 88 MB. On a fresh install their titles appear immediately; first Play prepares each private local copy. Updating an existing installation keeps its assignments and saved mix. The release verification record provides the exact IPA size and hash.

Before you start

Have the iPhone, its passcode, a USB data cable, an internet-connected Windows PC or Mac, the IPA, and access to your own Apple Account and any verification prompts. Enter account details only into the official signing software or Apple's authentication interface on your computer. Do not send them to the person sharing Undertone.

With Apple's free Personal Team provisioning, profiles expire after seven days. Apple permits three installed development apps per device and ten App IDs within a seven-day period. AltStore Classic itself occupies an installed-app slot. Direct AltServer installation avoids needing the AltStore app on the phone. Apple account limits, AltServer direct installation.

Windows: prepare the computer

  1. Download AltServer from altstore.io, extract its installer ZIP and run the included installer.
  2. Follow AltStore's prerequisites for Apple-distributed iTunes and iCloud, not their Microsoft Store replacements. Official links: iTunes 64-bit, iTunes 32-bit, iCloud installer linked by AltStore. These provide Apple components AltServer uses. If you already depend on Store iCloud, follow AltStore's documented alternate setup.
  3. Start AltServer with Run as administrator. Approve the normal Windows prompt and allow its requested private-network access. Its icon appears near the clock, possibly under the hidden-icons arrow.

Source: AltStore's Windows installation guide.

Mac: prepare the computer

  1. Download current AltServer for Mac from altstore.io. Its current installation guide requires macOS 11 or later.
  2. Move AltServer.app to Applications and open it. Look for its menu-bar icon.
  3. Current AltServer does not require the old Mail plug-in. If an old copy requests one, update AltServer first; use the official legacy instructions only for a deliberately older setup.

Sources: AltStore's Mac guide, AltServer release notes.

Connect and install from either computer

  1. Connect the unlocked iPhone by USB. Accept the computer-access prompt on your own computer and Trust This Computer on the iPhone; enter the phone's passcode there. Confirm the phone appears in iTunes on the Windows AltServer setup, or Finder on Mac. Apple's trust instructions.
  2. Windows: hold Shift while clicking AltServer's tray icon. Mac: hold Option while clicking its menu-bar icon. Select Sideload .ipa…, choose the iPhone when asked, and select Undertone-iOS.ipa.
  3. Supply your own Apple Account in AltServer, complete any account verification it requests, and wait for its success notice. Keep the phone connected and unlocked during installation. Direct sideload is officially supported without first installing AltStore. AltServer direct-sideload documentation.

Authorize the first launch on iPhone

  1. In Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, find the developer entry for your own signing account. Trust it and confirm the prompts. Older iOS versions may use “Profiles & Device Management.” This is the app's developer trust step, distinct from trusting the USB computer. AltStore's trust instructions.
  2. In Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode, enable Developer Mode, restart when requested, and confirm Enable / Turn On after restart using the device passcode. Apple may show this setting only after a development install or device pairing has been initiated. Apple's Developer Mode guide.
  3. Open Undertone and confirm Studio / Gallery / Saved HUD / Guide appear. Start with a low device volume and try one included recording using APP-GUIDE.md. Existing users can also check Help → Native library without deleting the installed app.

Developer Mode and developer trust authorize a development-signed app. They are not background-audio permissions. There is no special SoundCloud-style iOS permission that guarantees playback over every other app.

Renew free signing before it expires

For the direct method, repeat Sideload .ipa… with the same IPA and signing account before the seven-day period ends. Install over the existing Undertone; do not delete it first. Set your own reminder before day seven. An expired app needs renewed signing before it will open again. AltServer renewal requirement.

Optional: manage refresh through AltStore Classic

  1. In the ordinary AltServer menu, choose Install AltStore → your iPhone and sign with the same account. Complete trust and Developer Mode if needed.
  2. For wireless refresh, enable the phone's Wi-Fi sync in iTunes on Windows, or Show this iPhone when on Wi-Fi in Finder on Mac. Apply the setting. Keep AltServer running on a computer reachable from the phone; a USB connection is also usable. Windows setup, Mac setup.
  3. Save the IPA to Files on the phone. In AltStore Classic, open My Apps, use its + import action and choose that IPA. The official app's import action opens an IPA document picker. AltStore import implementation.
  4. Use My Apps → Refresh All while AltServer is reachable. Check the displayed expiry instead of assuming background refresh succeeded. AltStore refresh guide.

Update while keeping the old library

Keep the existing Undertone installed and keep copies of your original media. Use the same signing account, same signing tool and same app identifier as your previous install; do not enable a duplicate-app or custom-identifier option. Install the newer IPA over it. An accepted in-place update keeps the app's data container; changing signing identity can prevent that update or create a separate app. Apple's update documentation describes preservation of Documents and Library data. Apple's update-data guidance.

The update preserves the older native iPhone library and mix. Open Help (question-mark icon) → Native library to check your recordings and make additional assignments. Choose Return to Studio when finished; the studio reflects those native changes. Audio continues to use the native player, while the new gallery and visual network have their own saved settings. Do not uninstall as the first response to an update error.

Build 9 stores channel-cover bytes in separate picture records and retains the legacy saved mix for recovery. Keep the existing app installed during that migration. After installing, verify that the recordings and channel pictures return, change a level, close and reopen the app, and confirm that the change remains saved. These are useful checks on each recipient's own phone; the release notes distinguish automated coverage from physical-device testing.

Troubleshooting

  • Phone absent from AltServer: unlock it, reconnect with a cable that carries data, try another USB port and confirm it appears in the Apple device-management software first. Charging alone does not prove a data connection. Use Apple's device-detection guide.
  • Windows Apple component or sign-in error: confirm the iTunes/iCloud prerequisites, start AltServer as administrator and inspect the exact error. Use the latest official installer and AltStore troubleshooting. Do not apply random binary patches or share account details.
  • Internet drops when you plug in the iPhone / Wi-Fi icon becomes Ethernet: keep the PC on its usual Wi-Fi and turn off Personal Hotspot on the iPhone while installing over USB. A USB hotspot can become Windows' preferred internet connection; an exhausted hotspot allowance can then interrupt Apple sign-in. USB device transfer and hotspot internet are separate. If you rely on the phone for your only internet connection, restore a working internet connection before signing.
  • “The provided anisette data is invalid”: first restore a stable internet connection, check the PC's date/time, and update AltServer using the official download. Confirm the Apple-distributed iTunes/iCloud components are installed, then retry signing. This error concerns Apple authentication data. AltStore's error reference covers errors 2013 and 3021. Use the current installer from the official AltStore website; the version available to you may differ from the publisher's earlier installation.
  • “Could not find AltServer”: keep it running, use the same reachable private network, or connect USB. School/guest networks can isolate devices. Allow the specific AltServer firewall access instead of disabling the firewall. AltStore connectivity guidance.
  • “Untrusted Developer” / Developer Mode required: follow the two separate phone authorization steps above, then reopen the app.
  • Three-app or ten-App-ID limit: free signing has separate quotas. Remove an unneeded app only after protecting its data, or wait for App IDs to expire. Repeatedly inventing new bundle IDs does not fix the quota. Apple's limits.
  • App expires or update is rejected: renew with the same account and identity. Keep the installed app and its data while investigating. A friend cannot fix your signing by sending an IPA signed with their personal account.

Once it opens, test one file in the foreground, with the screen locked, and alongside the other app you actually use. A successful computer installation is separate from successful playback on your phone.

Install Undertone 3.2 on Android

Use the release file Undertone-Android.apk on Android 9 or later. The APK supports the normal Android CPU architectures without choosing a separate download. You do not need an Apple Account, AltServer or your own signing key. The main installation steps were checked against official guidance on November 9, 2026 (Denver).

Keep Android System WebView and/or Chrome updated on the phone. The studio interface uses the system WebView; the audio mixer runs in a native background service.

Install the APK

  1. Save the APK to the phone, extract the package if necessary, and open it from Downloads or Files.
  2. If Android blocks installation from that source, follow Settings → Allow from this source for the browser or file manager that opened the APK. You can also search Settings for Install unknown apps. This permission belongs to the source app, not every app on the phone.
  3. Return to the installer and tap Install, then Open. Afterward you may turn that source's installation permission off again. Google's path is Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps; manufacturer labels vary. Google's installation instructions.
  4. Leave Google Play Protect enabled. If it offers to scan the unfamiliar APK, let it scan and read the result. If it blocks the app as harmful, stop and send the exact message to the person providing the build; do not disable protection to get around it. Google Play Protect.
  5. Open Undertone and confirm Studio / Gallery / Saved HUD / Guide appear. Follow APP-GUIDE.md, starting with one included recording and a low device volume.

Work-managed, child-managed or protected devices may prohibit this installation route. Follow the device's applicable management policy. Some regions and Android releases also have developer-verification requirements; consult the current Android verification guidance if that is the message you see. A generic APK guide cannot override those requirements.

Install an update without clearing your library

Keep Undertone installed, open the newer APK, and accept Update. The publisher must retain the same package identity and signing certificate for an ordinary in-place update. Android compares the signatures; a different certificate will not update the existing installation. Android signing documentation.

Do not uninstall Undertone or select Clear storage / Clear data to resolve a signature mismatch. That can erase imports and settings. Keep original media copies and ask for a correctly signed update. An APK's signing certificate verifies its update identity; it does not mean the APK has been approved by Google Play.

Background playback and battery settings

Android audio runs in a native foreground playback service, with Play/Pause/Stop controls in its media notification. Recordings and generated tones continue independently of the visual WebView. The app pauses when headphones disconnect or it detects phone/communication mode. It does not request exclusive audio focus, allowing concurrent playback where the system and other app permit it.

First test playback with Undertone open. Then lock the phone for a short test and try the other media app you intend to use. Audio interruptions, calls, system audio policies and manufacturer battery controls can still affect playback; installing an APK does not guarantee uninterrupted mixing with every app.

If playback stops only after the phone sleeps, inspect Undertone's own app battery settings. On Pixel, look under Settings → Apps → App battery usage → Undertone → Allow background usage. Keep normal optimization unless a playback problem calls for changing Undertone's setting. Google's battery settings.

On Samsung, Settings → Apps → Undertone → Battery may offer Unrestricted. Also check Battery → Background usage limits for Sleeping or Deep sleeping apps. Make any exception for Undertone specifically; settings vary by model. Samsung's audio troubleshooting, Samsung's application-management guide.

Troubleshooting

  • “App not installed” / incompatible package: confirm the complete APK downloaded, enough storage is available and your phone meets the release's requirements. Compare the file against SHA256SUMS.txt. Keep the exact error for troubleshooting.
  • “Conflicts with an existing package”: check whether the installed copy came from a different signing key. Obtain the matching update; do not erase the existing library as a first step.
  • Install control unavailable: check the source-app permission and device-management restrictions. Keep Play Protect enabled.
  • Import fails: download the media fully onto the phone first, then select it again. An MP3/WAV filename does not guarantee a valid decodable file. For gallery video, both duration and codec must be supported.
  • Sound stops after Force stop: open Undertone and start playback again. Force stop ends the running app; it is not a pause control.

Read APP-GUIDE.md for channel controls, the gallery and the visual network.

Audio storage on Android

The Android build accepts mono or stereo MP3/WAV imports up to 1 GiB per encoded file. It decodes audio to local 48 kHz stereo PCM on disk, with a 4 GiB decoded-file cap (about 3.1 hours). This uses roughly 1.4 GB of storage per hour of audio, so long recordings need substantial free space even when the MP3 is small. The temporary encoded import is removed after decoding; retain your original files elsewhere. Android manages audio through the ten studio slots; the separate legacy Native library screen is an iPhone feature.

Included starter recordings

The 3.2 starter release includes five assigned MP3s totaling about 88 MB. On a fresh install their titles appear immediately; first Play prepares each private recording. Preparing all five adds about 1.5 GB of private decoded audio, in addition to the app and any installer retained in Downloads. The release verification record provides the exact APK size and hash. See APP-GUIDE.md for queued playback cancellation and update behavior.

Use Undertone 3.2

The expanded studio combines ten audio slots with a visual network and a private media collection. The flow diagram and intent text are visual organization tools. They do not route audio signals, change a recording's contents or establish a subliminal, medical or therapeutic effect.

First listen

  1. Lower your phone's output volume, open Undertone and allow the interface to finish loading.
  2. Choose one of the five starting tracks and tap Play. Allow its first-use preparation to finish. To add your own file instead, select Import audio or an empty channel and choose an MP3 or WAV; download cloud-provider files onto the phone first.
  3. Pause keeps the current position; Stop rewinds. Use the bottom transport to control the mix. Saved audio returns paused on a new launch.
  4. Add more recordings, up to ten slots. A slot can hold a recording, a generated tone or both. Importing into an occupied slot replaces that slot's recording.
  5. Tap a channel name or its controls button to adjust level, seek, mute, looping and stereo position. Optional automatic panning moves between its selected bounds.
  6. Test the app with the phone locked and with your usual second media app before relying on a long background session. Calls, alarms, device controls and other apps can interrupt playback. Use the platform installation guide if background playback stops.

The top navigation opens Studio, Gallery, Saved HUD and Guide. On a narrow screen within Studio, Flow, Channels and Media switch workspaces. The bottom master transport remains available.

On Android, the native media notification also provides Play/Pause/Stop. Headphone disconnection and phone/communication mode pause playback. Long imports decode to disk and can use about 1.4 GB per hour; see the Android storage limits before importing a large collection. The iPhone version also uses native audio; the visual interface is not responsible for keeping audio running while the phone sleeps.

Master level and generated tones

The master runs from 0% to 1,100%. At 100% it adds no master boost; 1,100% represents 11 times nominal digital amplitude before output limiting. It cannot make the phone's amplifier 11 times stronger, and an already-loud recording may gain little extra loudness because the limiter contains peaks. Set comfortable device volume first, then adjust the mix gradually.

Each slot's optional tone has its own level, stereo controls and carrier frequency up to 20,000 Hz. Carrier choices are sine, triangle, square, sawtooth, pulse, asymmetric, parabolic, sinc and fractal. Modulation changes the carrier's amplitude; it is separate from carrier frequency. Very high settings may be inaudible or limited by the phone and output device. No audible tone is not a reason to raise the output abruptly.

Create and edit the visual network

The network has 12 endpoints: Core, channels 1–10, and HUD. Empty channels can participate; adding or clearing a recording does not delete their visual routes.

The previous large, soft portal vortex fills the center and extends behind the audio cards. Your optional intent image stays small at the center. The larger vortex keeps the existing intent editing and channel/Core/HUD routes available; its appearance does not change audio playback.

  1. Tap the centered Set intent / Edit intent control to enter text and choose a color. You can also choose an intent image. The Core is surrounded by a fine white 64-cell tetrahedral grid; the grid is decorative and does not block connection controls.
  2. Turn on Link, choose an origin port, then choose one or more destinations. The origin remains selected for branching. You can also drag between ports, including from the Core's connection button or port.
  3. Core, channels and HUD can each be either origin or destination. Both directions in the connection dialog creates two directional links.
  4. Tap a curve to edit it, reverse its direction or remove it. A reverse changes the visual link, not audio playback. When the reverse direction already exists, reversing merges into that existing direction instead of creating a duplicate.
  5. Drag channel title bars to arrange the cards. The starting layout is balanced around the centered Core, with a centered Core ↔ HUD route. The Fit view icon, labeled Reset card positions for accessibility, restores the starting card positions and leaves your links intact.
  6. Use Apply to visualization to keep dialog changes, or Cancel to discard them. Canvas edits begin saving after each change.
  7. Clear links removes the visual routes only. Undo clear restores that cleared set without deleting routes you added afterward. It does not clear recordings or the saved intent. The undo action applies to the current Flow editing session, so restore before leaving or reloading if needed.

New networks start with Core → channels 1–5 and two-way HUD links to Core and all ten channels. These starting links are editable. The HUD links are added once when migrating an older graph; removed links stay removed on later launches.

In Motion settings, Reverse vortex changes the vortex's rotation and inward/outward movement. Speed, glow and visual pause are separate from link direction and audio transport. Pausing visuals does not pause the audio mix.

Gallery: images, GIFs and videos

Open Gallery or the compact media panel, then use Add media.

  • Up to 50 gallery items total, including 10 videos.
  • Each video must have a readable duration of 90 seconds or less and a codec supported by the device.
  • Select thumbnails, use previous/next, or swipe the main preview. Expand it for a larger view.
  • Enable Auto slideshow and enter a positive duration in seconds to cycle through items.
  • Videos play muted and inline, and pause when hidden or offscreen. They do not add their audio to your mix.
  • To set a channel cover, select an image or GIF, choose a channel and press Assign. Removing the gallery item does not remove an already assigned cover.

Invalid imports report their reason. Valid items in the same batch can still be accepted. A video counts toward both the 50-item total and the 10-video limit.

Five rotating backgrounds

The background manager holds five images or GIFs, each with its own positive display time. Choose a thumbnail, swipe, or use previous/next for manual selection. Edit each duration, then turn on Auto cycle to use those individual times.

At capacity, replace a selected background or remove one before adding another. Gallery slideshow timing and background timing are independent. The picture control in the top bar adjusts backdrop strength. The supplied wolf artwork provides the starting collection.

Intent-led mini HUD and full player

The mini Saved HUD has a reserved dock below the routing canvas. Curves approach its top port, leaving the player clear of the network. Its port accepts connections in both directions; each route can be reversed or removed like any other link. Use Open full Saved HUD for the larger player.

With Flow coupling on, intent text leads the HUD's palette, pace, glow and breathing. Local keyword profiles include Calm / sleep, Focus / clarity, Grounded / safe, Energy / confidence, Release / growth and Love / connection. Unmatched text receives a repeatable Custom text appearance. These labels describe a local visual mapping, not an interpretation of your mind or a claim that the animation produces those outcomes. The readout shows which visual profile was selected. Routes, active audio and the flow settings add smaller visual accents.

The embedded player keeps the evolving effect visible instead of fading away after five seconds. Play HUD / Pause HUD controls its animation separately from audio playback. Flow coupling off restores the original style and pace while continuous visibility remains enabled. The mini view pauses when offscreen, the page is hidden or visual motion is paused. Mini and full views are separate animation instances; leaving the full screen closes that instance.

The full tab retains the Teal Apparitions player's controls, including Captured moment, Restart from zero, orbit, zoom, import/export and fullscreen. Captured moment restores the saved camera and timeline while continuous visibility remains enabled. In the phone apps, Fullscreen fills the app viewport and Exit fullscreen returns to the normal view. The browser preview also offers Open original for the untouched saved player. The separate Aether art layer has been removed.

Capture a HUD image

  1. Open Saved HUD and allow the artwork to finish loading. Pause HUD can hold a moment for capture.
  2. Choose Capture image. The current HUD artwork is saved as a PNG in the app's local Gallery and counts toward its 50-item limit.
  3. Choose View captured image to open Gallery. You can use the saved image like another gallery image, including assigning a channel cover or background through the normal media controls.
  4. The browser preview additionally offers Download PNG. The native phone app saves into its own Gallery; it does not promise a separate Photos export.

If the player is still loading, frame access is unavailable or Gallery is busy/full, wait or free a Gallery slot and retry. Capturing an image does not record or change audio.

Guide: playlists and optional recordings

Use the creator's own download links and terms. A free-to-watch video does not establish that a downloadable file exists.

Keep the supplied five starters and use slots 6–10 for optional additions. Direct watch URLs, uploader identity and free-file availability for those additional titles were not verified. Channel-cover storage and recovery

Channel pictures accept readable images up to 20 MB and are stored separately from the mix settings. The mix keeps a reference to each picture; volume, position and seek changes do not rewrite its image bytes. Existing readable covers are migrated, and the older saved mix record is retained for recovery.

If an individual cover cannot be recovered, the app keeps its earlier record, shows fallback artwork and reports the channel in a warning. An unreadable cover is handled separately from the audio settings. Keep the installed app and original media while that warning is investigated; do not uninstall or clear storage to remove it.

The separate picture store concerns channel covers. Gallery, video, background and intent-image storage formats remain unchanged; the Gallery still holds 50 items including up to ten videos of 90 seconds or less. The larger portal vortex remains unchanged. See the release notes for the completed automated persistence checks and physical-device testing limits.

Privacy and keeping your media

Imports and mix settings belong to this local app installation. There is no Undertone account or media-upload service. The shared starter recordings are part of the public installer; your later imports, mix and intent remain local. The operating system's own backup and file-provider behavior is separate.

Keep original files outside the app. Uninstalling, clearing app storage, signing as a different app identity or changing devices can remove or separate local data. Before updating, keep the current app installed and follow the platform guide. Do not treat an installer ZIP as a backup of your imported library.
